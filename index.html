How It Works

Getting your car fixed shouldn't be complicated. Here's what to expect when you bring your vehicle to Nash's.

1

Call or Schedule

Give us a call at (586) 254-3200 or drop by. We'll get you in quickly and discuss what's going on with your vehicle.

2

Full Diagnosis

Our ASE-certified technicians perform a thorough inspection using the latest diagnostic equipment to identify the root cause.

3
